 nolysis, and subsequently dehydrated to terephthalonitrile (TPN), the key intermediate for PXDA synthesis.
 Successful separation of each intermediate ensures efficient hydrogenation of TPN to PXDA, achieving an overall
 PXDA yield of 54.5 % from PET. Furthermore, the transformation of common real plastic waste and scale-up
 experiments demonstrate the robustness and substantial industrial potential of this catalytic route. This study
 establishes a viable and promising strategy for upcycling PET waste into high value-added product PXDA,
 significantly broadening the scope of PET-derived chemicals and stimulating further exploration of other valu­
 able derivatives.
